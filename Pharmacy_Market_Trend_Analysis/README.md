# 💊 Pharmacy Market Trend Analysis (SQL)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores pharmacy market dynamics using structured SQL analysis.

The goal is to understand how pharmacy availability is changing over time and what this may imply for healthcare accessibility and market structure.

The analysis focuses on operational trends, business impact, and structural shifts within the pharmacy sector.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

* How are pharmacy openings and closures evolving over time?
* Are closures disproportionately affecting certain pharmacy sizes?
* What are the implications for healthcare access?
* Are distance sellers replacing physical pharmacies?

---

## 🧰 Tools & Techniques

* Microsoft SQL Server
* Aggregations and grouped analysis
* Window functions for cumulative trends
* CROSS APPLY for category comparison
* Business-oriented analytical thinking

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains monthly pharmacy activity, including:

* Openings and closures by pharmacy size
* Net market change
* Total pharmacy count
* Distance seller activity

---

## 📈 Analytical Focus

The analysis aims to identify:

* Long-term market contraction or growth
* Structural differences between small, medium, and large pharmacies
* Potential accessibility risks linked to pharmacy closures
* Whether digital distribution channels are offsetting physical declines
